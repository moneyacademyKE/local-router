## Objective

Provide `infered/auto`, ranking the eight models in the owner's 2026-11-10 comparison by the recorded Artificial Analysis intelligence scores, with hourly market membership refresh and output-token asks below $1 per million. Retry another chain member when the precise observed Codex credit-exhaustion text appears as the initial answer.

## Implementation

- `src/router/auto.js` contains the scored comparison roster and hourly snapshot refresh. A D1 row and Cloudflare's `0 * * * *` trigger share the snapshot; requests can refresh it when missing. `GET /v1/auto` exposes the current list.
- `migrations/0002_auto_chain.sql` creates the D1 row. Fresh authenticated market data is required; stale data is not silently reused across hourly buckets.
- A per-attempt InferHub `x-max-output-price: 0.999999` bid enforces a rate strictly below $1/M even if the cheapest listed offer disappears. A caller may set a lower cap. This is an output rate, not a total cost or input-price cap. Fallbacks never drop it.
- `src/router/quota.js`, `client.js`, and `stream.js` recognize the reported Codex credit refusal as an upstream failure instead of an answer, including when split across SSE events. The request proceeds to another model. Heartbeats remain live; tool-only completions continue to work.
- `test/auto_test.clj`, `test/auto_worker_test.clj`, `test/quota_test.clj`, `test/quota_worker_test.clj` and the runner cover the boundary and failover. `test/worker_test.clj` checks that auto appears in the catalog.
- The OpenCrabs tupesa picker was updated using `config_manager`; its existing default remains `infered/terra-kimi`.

## Boundaries and residual risk

The scores are a dated benchmark snapshot, not hourly refreshed benchmark data, and vary by reasoning setting. Cheapest observed asks are market observations, not a promise of seller capacity or account access. Cloudflare accepted the scheduled trigger; an actual future scheduled firing was not observed during this deployment. Price changes within an hour are still constrained by InferHub's per-attempt bid, but list membership changes on refresh. The live provider did not produce a credit-exhaustion incident during verification; that branch was verified deterministically against the Worker rather than asserted from live data. A fallback cannot safely replace genuine text already delivered to the client, and shared exhaustion of all candidate providers still fails. An output-price ceiling does not bound input charges, total tokens, or fees. No load test was run for the new scheduled refresh under Cloudflare Free CPU constraints.
